How to use this thing
1. Open up a terminal window (On Macs, open Terminal.app)
   (One extra caution, if the terminal says "zsh: permission denied: .../Desktop/FuckSetupApp_v3.1/fsa" or anything like this, enter "chmod -R 755" and press space, then drag n' drop your FuckSetupApp folder in terminal window, press space)
2. Input cd and press space, drag n' drop your FuckSetupApp folder in terminal window
3. Press Enter, then run './fsa'
4. Follow those guides written in the script.

Q11: What do you mean 'Waveshare'?
A11: Like said in the README, to boot a ramdisk requires your device to enter pwnDFU mode. And that requires a valid BootROM exploit. For A12/A13 devices, there's a different BootROM exploit called 'usbliter8' (More specified infos here: github.com/prdgmshift/usbliter8). Instead of using checkm8, limera1n or whatever, the 'usbliter8' case requires an extra piece of hardware called a debug board. Most people recommend using a Waveshare RP2350-USB-A board, and that's where the 'Waveshare' comes from.

A12: Well in my country it needs roughly $4 (¥30), try googling 'ebay waveshare RP-2350-USB-A', you'll find it out.
